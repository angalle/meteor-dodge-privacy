Public hosting for **Meteor Dodge** (Android, Google Play) legal pages.

- Privacy policy: https://angalle.github.io/meteor-dodge-privacy/

Contact: use the "Developer contact" link on the Google Play listing (the store page is the canonical contact channel; we do not publish a personal email address here).
